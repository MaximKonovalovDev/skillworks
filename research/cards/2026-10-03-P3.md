  `pypdf/_text_extraction/_layout_mode/_fixed_width_page.py`). Our
  `book2skill/extract.py::_read_pdf` calls plain `page.extract_text()`
  per page in index order only — no layout mode for tables/code blocks.
  Reference: `https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf/blob/main/pypdf/_page.py`.

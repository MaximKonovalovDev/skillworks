## Pipeline truth (graded cost audit + fingerprint lock, not token sums)

- Donor grades without a model: cost model is always-loaded (name+description
  every session) vs on-trigger (SKILL.md body when it fires) vs on-demand
  (references/ only when the body sends you there); rules are arithmetic +
  pattern-matching with stable rule IDs, severity (error/warning/note), message
  + remedy on every finding; body budget 2000 tokens, description 40–1024
  chars, Jaccard overlap 0.34 over distinctive terms; lock side is FNV-1a
  fingerprint + YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ stamp, no dependency.
- Ours audits chars//4 per .md with no rules/grades and refreshes on sha256
  of chunks/*.txt with a bare lock string. Gap: no routability/routing/size
  grades, no remedy, no versioned lock with source+hash.

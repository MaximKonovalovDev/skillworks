## Market truth (what sells, what format wins)

- Registry: ClawHub is the public OpenClaw skill/plugin registry at clawhub.ai
  (docs: "Skills: versioned text bundles with `SKILL.md` plus supporting
  files"). Format that wins = SKILL.md bundle + semver + tags (`latest`) +
  changelog — exactly our skill-pack shape (T-01).
- Listing signals that sell: downloads, stars, installs, verified/official
  badge, version, tags, README preview, security-scan summary (docs "What
  ClawHub hosts" table; donor normalizes `stars ← metrics.bookmarks`,
  `installs ← metrics.rolling60DayInstalls`, `verified ← official`).
- Scale signal: marketplace surged 2,000 → 50,000 skills (cybersecuritynews,
  2026) with 1,184 confirmed-malicious-skill supply-chain wave (CSA, Feb–May
  2026) — our P5 shop lane needs scan-summary + verified-badge equivalents
  before any listing (ties to `audit` stage + eval gate 0.6).

## Eval truth (what the best do that we do not)

- `ai-evos/agent-skills` (Apache-2.0, live, named best): two-stage model-graded eval
  (`shared/eval_framework.py`: `generate_agent_response` subject + `grade_response`
  grader, per-criterion pass/partial/fail → 1.0/0.5/0.0, `weighted_score =
  sum(score*weight)`, scenario pass `>= 0.7`); every scenario runs twice —
  skill-equipped vs bare (`baseline=True`, system=None) — reporting lift
  (93.2% vs 81.4%, +11.8pp across 201 scenarios); dual reports
  (`shared/scoring.py`: `aggregate_scores` overall + by_difficulty + by_category →
  `generate_json_report` + `generate_markdown_report`); interpretation bands
  85/70/50. Our `book2skill/eval.py` (57 lines) is binary must-word hits,
  report `{total, passed, rate}` only, no weights, no baseline, no breakdown.
  Reference: `https://github.com/ai-evos/agent-skills/blob/main/shared/eval_framework.py`.
- `stanfordnlp/dspy` (MIT, live): `Evaluate` class (`dspy/evaluate/evaluate.py`:
  `devset` + `metric` → `EvaluationResult(score, results)`, per-item
  `(example, prediction, score)`, `score = round(100*ncorrect/ntotal, 2)`,
  `failure_score=0.0` on exception, `max_errors`, `save_as_csv`/`save_as_json`,
  `display_table`, `Average Metric: ncorrect / ntotal` log). Our `run_eval`
  returns `{skill, total, passed, rate}` only, no per-item rows, no failure
  score, no saved detail. Idea: `EvaluationResult` shape + failure handling.
  Reference: `https://github.com/stanfordnlp/dspy/blob/main/dspy/evaluate/evaluate.py`.
- `EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness` (MIT, live, riser): metric/aggregation
  registry (`lm_eval/api/metrics.py`: `@register_metric(metric,
  higher_is_better, output_type, aggregation)` + `@register_aggregation`
  `mean/median/perplexity/f1/bleu/chrf/ter/brier_score`, passthrough `*_fn`
  items + `exact_match_hf_evaluate` with ignore-case/punct/numbers, `mean_stderr`
  + bootstrap `stderr_for_metric`, `pooled_sample_stderr` /
  `combined_sample_stderr` for groups). Our eval has one hardcoded metric
  (all-must-words) with no registry, no `higher_is_better`, no stderr.
  Idea: registry + mean/stderr aggregation for the gate report.
  Reference: `https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/blob/main/lm_eval/api/metrics.py`.

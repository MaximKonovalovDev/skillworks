## Try this (from the skill)

1. Condensation — one manifest image carries several latent dream-thoughts at once. Ask: "what is condensation, and how does it relate to latent dream-thoughts?"
2. Displacement — psychical intensity shifts off the important thought onto something trivial in the manifest content. Ask: "how does displacement shift intensity to the manifest content?"
3. Manifest vs latent — the remembered dream (manifest) is a distorted version of the hidden thoughts (latent). Ask: "what is the difference between manifest content and latent dream-thoughts?"
4. Censorship — a resisting force distorts the latent thoughts so the manifest dream looks innocent. Ask: "what role does censorship play in distorting the manifest dream?"
5. Infantile wishes — the motive force of the dream is an infantile wish, often anxious on the surface. Ask: "why are infantile wishes central to dream interpretation?"

Stuck? Start with `chapters/notes.md`, then the chunk in `references/sources.md`
matching the task; quote the source chunk, never invent beyond it.

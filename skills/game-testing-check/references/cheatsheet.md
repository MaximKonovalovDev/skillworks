# Cheatsheet: game-testing-check

Playtest: `one-on-one`, `focus group` (5 to 10), `extended playtest` (days). `moderator` guides and `takes notes`. Wear the `player shoes`. Recruit the `target audience` via `screening surveys`; players `not biased`. Build is `smooth experience` and `bug-free`. Cover the `tutorial`. Score `fun factor` and `difficulty balancing`.

Perf: `stress testing` with `multiple players`; `load testing` can be `automated` with `backend developers`. Watch `performance under stress`: `frame rate` and `lag`. Check `compatibility` and `installability`. Keep `test sets` for `hardware compatibility`. Watch `scalability`.

Process: `test charter` for `exploratory testing`; `pairwise testing` (`two heads`); `risk identification` then `risk analysis`; `smoke testing`, `beta testing`, `ad hoc`; `repro rate` and `bug flow`.

# Playtest matrix

Own-words checklist. No book text is copied.

| Format | Size | When | Pass row |
|---|---|---|---|
| `one-on-one` | 1 player + 1 `moderator` | early read on tutorial and feel | moderator guides, asks, and `takes notes`, recording on |
| `focus group` | 5 to 10 testers | compare reactions in one room | every voice heard, no star tester dominates |
| `extended playtest` | small set, several days | retention and balance over time | day-1 to day-3 notes show progress and return intent |

Rules:

- The `moderator` is often a `UX designer` or someone close to the design. The moderator guides the player and `takes notes` while recording runs.
- Tester mindset: wear the `player shoes`. Judge flow and feel as a user, not as QA.
- Recruit the right `target audience` with `screening surveys`. Players are `not biased` toward the company or the game.
- Build readiness: a `smooth experience` that is `bug-free` enough that notes judge the game, not crashes.
- Always cover the `tutorial`: a new player finishes it with no outside help.
- Score the `fun factor` and `difficulty balancing` per level: note too easy, fair, or too hard, with one line why.
